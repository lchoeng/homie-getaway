# Homie Getaway

An auto-runner where you pick a homie (Boomer, Mike, Matt, David, Louie, Anthony or Loni) and run from the cops.

Open `index.html` in a browser (works on phone and desktop).

- 4 areas: Back Alleys, The Park, The Mall, The Streets
- Jump over trash cans, fences, bushes, shopping carts, boxes, plants, wet floor signs and cones
- Slide under clotheslines, branches, sale banners and awnings
- Jump the dogs that chase you from behind
- Grab cash for your stash, and fresh white kicks to run faster and smash through obstacles
- Hit too much stuff and the cop catches you: BUSTED!

Controls: Up / Space to jump (again in the air to double jump), Down to slide, Enter to select, M for music. On a phone use the JUMP and SLIDE buttons.
